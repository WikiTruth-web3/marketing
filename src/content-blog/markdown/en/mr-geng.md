## One Person, One Computer, A Massive "Earthquake" in Academia

Recently, academic anti-fraud blogger "Mr. Geng" single-handedly reported 5 "Outstanding Youth" (recipients of the National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars) and deans from universities such as Tongji University, Nankai University, and Sun Yat-sen University for severe academic fraud in the main journal or sub-journals of *Nature*.

This blogger was once a PhD student majoring in Biomedical Engineering at Beihang University. Two years ago, due to academic unfairness and his supervisor delaying his graduation, he ultimately chose to drop out and embarked on the path of fighting academic fraud. Relying solely on a computer, a few AI image duplication checking algorithms, and a statistical model, he sent chills down the spines of top scholars in the entire life science field in just 30 days. Various universities immediately launched investigations, and some of the deans involved have been dismissed or demoted in technical rank.

## The Dilemma of a Lone Hero: The Price of Truth

However, right after his first anti-fraud video was published, Mr. Geng received over 100 reports of fraud, but simultaneously faced immense real-world pressure.

According to *Sanlian Lifeweek*, someone called to probe him "if there is room for negotiation, and what kind of needs you might have." His family was also extremely worried about him facing retaliation and bitterly urged him to stop speaking out. Ultimately, Mr. Geng helplessly stated: "After this round of anti-fraud, I probably won't continue."

Mr. Geng's experience reveals two fatal flaws in the current academic circle and even the entire social supervision system:

1. **The cost of doing evil is extremely low**: Under the distorted evaluation system of "up or out" and pure project-orientation, fraud has become a shortcut to acquiring tens of millions in funding and the title of "Outstanding Youth."
2. **The cost of exposing is extremely high**: Traditional "whistleblowers" must show their true faces or easily traceable digital identities, facing the joint strangulation of power and capital alone. Not only do they get zero economic return, but even their personal safety and careers cannot be guaranteed.

When the cost of maintaining the truth is far higher than the cost of covering up lies, bad money driving out good becomes inevitable.
